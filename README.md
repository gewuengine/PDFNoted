**PDFnoted** is a free, browser-based PDF workspace with a unique AI compliance audit engine. Unlike traditional PDF tools that only edit and convert, PDFnoted reviews your contracts and policy documents against **174+ regulations** across 11 industries and 3 jurisdictions (EU, US, China).

---

## 🛡️ What Makes It Different

### AI Compliance Audit (The Moat)
Upload a contract or policy document → AI reviews every clause → flags risks → cites specific legal provisions → suggests fixes.

**Coverage:**
- **AI Law**: EU AI Act (2024), China Generative AI Interim Measures, US TAKE IT DOWN Act (2025)
- **Cross-border Data**: GDPR Arts. 44-49, PIPL Arts. 38-43, China Data Export Security Assessment
- **ESG Reporting**: EU CSRD (2024), ISSB Standards, China Sustainable Disclosure Standards (2024)

**Engine specs**: 14,901 parsed legal rules, multi-level retrieval (exact match → TF-IDF → citation expansion → authority reranking)

### 23 Free PDF Tools (Browser-side, No Upload)
All tools run entirely in your browser — files never leave your device:
- **Convert**: PDF ↔ Word/Excel/PPT/JPG/PNG/Text/Markdown
- **Organize**: Merge, Split, Reorder, Rotate, Crop, Delete pages
- **Secure**: Password protect, Unlock, Redact (AI-powered PII masking), E-sign
- **Professional**: Watermark, Page numbers, Headers/Footers, Bates numbering, Flatten
- **Advanced**: OCR, PDF Compare, AI Translate, Image → PDF, Extract images, Metadata editor

### WYSIWYG PDF Editor + AI Polish
Click text to edit inline, add images, then use AI to polish, proofread, or translate — everything exports with embedded CJK fonts (Source Han Sans).
